# Privacy Policy — Dojo

_Last updated: October 2026_

## 1. Overview
Dojo is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data necessary to operate the app.

## 2. Data We Collect
- **Device ID**: A randomly generated anonymous identifier stored locally on your device. Used for leaderboard ranking only.
- **Skill Rating & game history**: Stored locally on your device via AsyncStorage.
- **Leaderboard data**: If you complete a Daily session, your anonymous device ID, SR, level, and streak are submitted to our leaderboard (Supabase). No personal information is included.
- **Analytics**: We use PostHog to collect anonymous usage events (app opens, session completions, share actions). No personally identifiable information is collected.

## 3. Data We Do NOT Collect
- Name, email, or any personal identifiers
- Location data
- Financial information
- Wallet addresses (unless you voluntarily connect a Solana wallet)

## 4. Solana Wallet
Connecting a Solana wallet is optional. If connected, your wallet address is used only as your leaderboard identifier and displayed in shortened form (e.g., `Sol…1337`).

## 5. Third-Party Services
- **Supabase** (leaderboard storage): supabase.com/privacy
- **PostHog** (analytics): posthog.com/privacy

## 6. Data Retention
Anonymous leaderboard entries are retained until you request deletion.
